02 — Strategy

How they built the playbook

Before launch · 6 months pre-product
Community before product  SME SMO

6 months building an audience before manufacturing a single pair. Product direction was shaped by community input.

Content-first Instagram SMO

"Badass Indians" — celebrating under-the-radar Indian stories before any product existed. Profile optimised around cultural keywords.

Community feedback loops SME

Founders posted manifestos, asked questions, ran polls. First buyers became co-creators. Product shaped by the people who'd buy it.

Manifesto SEO content SEO

Long-form content on India's invisibility in global sneaker culture indexed early. Targeted "Indian streetwear" and "desi sneaker brand" keywords.

Pre-launch tracking GA4

GA4 on a pre-launch page. Events tracked: email sign-ups, "Notify Me" clicks. Gave demand evidence before production decisions.

Launch · Diwali drop framing
Don't launch a product — tell a story  SME SMO

Debut collection framed as a Diwali story. Every element referenced Indian culture. Vogue feature and a viral Reel came within the first few months.

Cultural narrative Reels SMO

Reels as stories: karigar craft, symbolism behind motifs, the meaning of "Gully." One went viral — tracked via GA4 UTM links to a D2C traffic spike.

Vogue India feature SME

Landed Vogue India through a genuine cultural authenticity pitch — not paid placement. Community reshared widely, generating thousands of organic mentions.

Drop pages as editorial SEO

Each drop had a story page, not just a listing. Diwali launch included cultural context, design notes and karigar profiles — ranked for long-tail keywords months later.

Founder-led content SME

Arjun's posts about 6 months of research and failed prototypes drove massive DM volume and press inbound from Inc42, GQ and others.

Growth · Collabs & cultural drops

Per-drop GA4 funnels GA4

Each drop had its own event stream: content_view → product_page → add_to_cart → purchase. UTMs tracked which media drove actual conversions vs only awareness.

Scale · Shark Tank + Series A

Shark Tank India S5 — ₹1 Cr from Aman Gupta at ₹175 Cr valuation. GA4 audience geography guided which cities got offline stores first.

Pitch clip posted to Instagram with cultural context. Follower growth spiked in 48 hours. Bio link updated to D2C store with GA4 UTM tracking.

07 — Key takeaways

What marketers can learn

01

Build community before product SME

6 months of audience-building before manufacturing meant Gully Labs launched to a waiting, invested community.

02

Cultural specificity beats vague storytelling SMO

The Hockey Drop worked because it picked one specific, under-told story — not a broad "India pride" campaign.

03

Collabs compound for SEO SEO

Every collab generated press. Every press piece was a backlink. Authority that paid ads can't replicate.

04

GA4 tells you where to expand GA4

Offline expansion followed data — not instinct. Highest repeat-purchase cities got the first physical touchpoints.

05

Story-first LTV is higher GA4

GA4 cohorts proved story-first customers have higher lifetime value — this data justified the no-discount model to Series A investors.

06

UTM tag everything GA4

Without UTM tags in GA4, high-performing partnerships are invisible. Attribution is only possible if you build it from day one.

02 — Strategy

The 60-day digital sprint

Week 1–2 · Foundation
Fix what Google sees first  SEO GA4

Before spending on ads or posting content, we fixed the infrastructure. A broken GMB listing was losing warm leads every day.

GMB complete overhaul SEO

Category: "Hair salon" + "Beauty salon." 28 photos added. Opening hours, price range, service menu and 8 FAQ answers filled from day one.

Landing page + schema SEO

H1: "Unisex Hair & Beauty Salon in Bhandup West." LocalBusiness schema added. Mobile speed optimised. Meta description written for click-through.

GA4 event setup GA4

Custom events: whatsapp_booking_click, gmb_call_click, gmb_directions. UTM parameters on every ad and social bio link from day one.

Instagram profile rebuild SMO

Bio: "Bhandup's favourite unisex salon 💇 | Book via WhatsApp." Highlights: Services / Pricing / Before & After / Reviews. Link-in-bio = WhatsApp appointment link.

Week 3–4 · Meta Ads launch
Hyper-local Meta campaigns  Meta Ads GA4

Three campaigns simultaneously — each targeting a different objective. All geo-targeted within 5 km of Bhandup West station.

Campaign 1 — Awareness Meta Ads

Objective: Reach. Audience: Women 18–40, interests "hair care," "beauty." Creative: before/after carousel. Budget: ₹150/day.

Campaign 2 — Bookings Meta Ads

Objective: WhatsApp Messages. Audience: Men + women 22–45, 3 km. Creative: "First haircut ₹199." CTA: Book Now on WhatsApp. Budget: ₹200/day.

Campaign 3 — Retargeting Meta Ads

Custom audience: website visitors + IG engagers, last 14 days. Creative: testimonial + offer reminder. Budget: ₹100/day.

GA4 ad tracking GA4

UTM on all ad links: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=booking_offer. GA4 showed which campaign drove WhatsApp clicks vs GMB direction requests.

Week 3–6 · Content & organic
Content for local discovery  SMO SEO

4 posts/week. Every post location-tagged Bhandup West. Reels at 8 PM weekdays. GMB posts updated weekly.

Before/After Reels SMO

Weekly transformation video posted with client consent. These consistently got the most saves and "Which stylist was this?" DMs that opened booking conversations.

Hyper-local hashtags SMO

#BhandupSalon, #BhandupWestMumbai, #MulundSalon alongside broader #MumbaiSalon. Micro-local tags drove profile visits from people within dine-in distance.

Review generation SEO

QR code at payment counter: "30 seconds on Google means the world to us." Staff prompted after each appointment. 0 to 41 reviews in 5 weeks, 4.5★ average.

GMB weekly posts SEO

GMB posts every Tuesday: fresh photos, offers, stylist spotlights. Regular posting signals freshness to Google's local ranking algorithm.

Week 7–8 · Retention
Turn first-timers into regulars  SMO GA4

With new clients coming in, focus shifted to bringing them back. WhatsApp follow-up and a simple referral incentive.

WhatsApp follow-up SMO

10 days after visit: "Hope you loved your [service]! Book your next visit and get 10% off." 28% replied and rebooked.

Referral card SMO

"Refer a friend — both get ₹100 off." Card given at checkout. No app, no tracking link — just a code name on the card. Trackable by front desk.

Retention cohort GA4

Organic Instagram clients had 52% 30-day return rate vs Meta paid clients at 34%. This validated keeping organic content investment even while scaling paid spend.

Lookalike audience Meta Ads

Uploaded 160 WhatsApp contacts as a Meta Custom Audience. Lookalike audience built on this. CPA for new bookings dropped 38% vs cold targeting.

03 — Meta Advertising  Meta Ads

Ad strategy & sample creatives

Targeting logic

Who we showed ads to

All campaigns geo-fenced within 3–5 km of Bhandup West station. Age: 18–45. Interests: "hair care," "beauty tips," "skin care routine," "salons." Income proxy: middle-income localities in the Mulund–Bhandup belt.

Budget breakdown

₹13,500/month · 60-day total ₹27,000

₹4,500 — Awareness (reach, brand recall)
₹6,000 — Conversions (WhatsApp bookings)
₹3,000 — Retargeting. 84 confirmed new clients. CPA: ₹321.

Dual tracking

Meta Pixel + GA4

Meta Pixel on landing page + GA4 UTM on all ad links. Meta reported 112 conversions. GA4 tracked 92. 18% gap explained by Meta's view-through attribution — running both prevented overspending on a false signal.
