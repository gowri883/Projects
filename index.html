Amazon PPC
Pay-Per-Click on Amazon
You pay only when a shopper clicks your sponsored ad. No click = no charge.
ACOS
Advertising Cost of Sale
Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue × 100. Lower = more efficient. Target: 20–35%.
ROAS
Return on Ad Spend
Ad Revenue ÷ Ad Spend. 3:1 or higher is the standard target.
CTR
Click-Through Rate
Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100. Industry benchmark: 3–5% on Amazon.

02 — Campaign Strategy

4-phase Amazon PPC approach

Week 1–2 · Preparation
Listing optimisation before ads  SEO PPC

PPC spend without a strong listing = wasted clicks. Listing optimisation came first.

Product listing SEO SEO

Title: primary keyword in first 80 characters. Bullet points: benefit-led copy with keyword variants. Backend search terms: 250 bytes of additional keywords. 9 images including lifestyle and detail shots.

Keyword research PPC

Tools: Helium 10 Cerebro, Amazon Brand Analytics. Identified 45 keywords across 3 match types: Broad ("garlic peeler"), Phrase ("electric garlic peeler"), Exact ("automatic garlic peeler").

Week 2 · Campaign launch
Auto + manual targeting together  PPC

Auto targeting to discover new search terms. Manual targeting to control spend on known high-intent keywords.

Auto targeting campaign PPC

Let Amazon match to relevant searches. Higher initial spend but invaluable for discovering what real customers actually search. Mined search term reports weekly for new keyword opportunities.

Manual targeting campaign PPC

30 hand-picked keywords from research. Starting bid $0.75 across all. Individual bids adjusted weekly based on ACOS performance per keyword.

Negative keyword list PPC

Added from week one: "cheap garlic peeler," "DIY garlic peeler," "garlic powder," "garlic puree." Excluded irrelevant traffic that eats budget without converting.

Search term report mining GA4

Downloaded weekly. Identified actual shopper queries triggering ads. Moved high-converting search terms into exact match campaigns. Excluded non-converters as negatives.

Week 3–12 · Optimisation
Weekly bid adjustments based on ACOS  PPC

Bid management is the core skill in Amazon PPC. Every keyword treated as its own investment.

Bid adjustment logic PPC

ACOS > target: lower bid by 10–15%. ACOS < target: increase bid by 10–20%. CTR < 3%: review listing images and title. Impression share < 50%: increase bid.

Real bid example — "Electric Garlic Peeler" PPC

Week 1: $0.75 bid · ACOS 45% → lowered. Week 2: $0.65 · ACOS 40% → adjusted. Week 3: $0.70 · ACOS 25% ✓ → held. Week 4+: $0.70 optimal.

Campaign expansion at day 30 PPC

After 30 days of data: created separate campaigns per keyword group (branded, competitor, generic). Added Sponsored Brands with logo + headline. Sponsored Display for retargeting page visitors.

06 — Takeaways

Key learnings from Amazon PPC

01

Fix the listing before running ads SEO

PPC drives clicks — a weak listing wastes them. Title, images and bullet points must convert before scaling spend.

02

Negative keywords protect your budget PPC

Broad targeting picks up "garlic powder" and "garlic puree" — completely different products. Negative keywords stop budget drain from day one.

03

Search term reports are gold PPC

Real shopper language surfaces in search term reports. Moving high-converters to exact match is how ACOS drops from 45% to 27%.

04

PPC directly lifts organic rank SEO

Amazon rewards sales velocity. PPC-driven sales moved organic ranking from page 8 to page 2. PPC and SEO aren't separate on Amazon — they compound.

05

Adjust bids weekly, not daily PPC

Daily adjustments create noise — Amazon's algorithm needs time to respond. Weekly reviews with 10–15% bid changes give cleaner data and steadier ACOS trends.

06

ACOS target depends on margin PPC

30% ACOS only works if product margin supports it. Calculate break-even ACOS first: (profit ÷ price) × 100. Never set a target without knowing your margin.
